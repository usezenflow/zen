Widgets

Widgets only read; the phone owns the layout and refreshes on its own clock, so every widget shows a time, not a live count. Colour lives in the same deep line-field art palettes as the app (chosen per task kind via artOf), with Bricolage Grotesque display titles and numbers over Be Vietnam Pro rows, and the orange ensō koi mark on paper and ink cards. Tapping a widget opens the matching view; the checkbox in a row ticks in place.
