Settings

One screen, Vietnamese only. Theme is pills. Online (timetable, exams, marks, fees) and LMS (assignments) are separate sections: each connects inline, keeps its password on the phone and disconnects quietly below. The card is the account; under it, one quiet row per part. The circle on a row asks the campus again; Điểm and Học phí open their own screen when tapped. Nothing calls the campus directly: every refresh, by hand or on a schedule, becomes a job in a small queue on the phone. One job runs at a time with a short pause between requests, a failed one waits and retries on its own, and asking twice for the same thing is one job. The queue survives closing the app and keeps going in the background. The last row is the version; when a newer release is on GitHub it says so and opens the update dialog. Zen checks on launch and on return (at most every 15 minutes) and offers each new version once a run. After an update, the first launch shows what is new, once. The installed version's notes ship inside the app (from CHANGELOG.md), so they need no network; only a newer release's notes come from GitHub.
