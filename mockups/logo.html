Ensō koi

A brush ring drawn in one stroke. The blunt start is the koi's head with its eye knocked out; the stroke thins as it turns, then flicks into a split tail just short of the head, so the circle never closes and the day stays open. Dry-brush texture inside the stroke is the same fine line language as the art.

2. States

The same mark, three moods. Loading: the ring turns once every six seconds and the brush breathes (still under reduced motion). Empty: the outline only, nothing to fill it today. Error: the mark tips back and the tail splits wider, so it reads as off without a warning icon.
