Capture: voice, type, photo

The mic stays the one button. Press it and it listens, as today. Drag your thumb up-left or up-right and two more ways to add things fan out: Ảnh (read a photo: a notice, a whiteboard, a printed timetable) and Gõ (type it, for a quiet room). Let go on one to pick it. Let go anywhere else and it was just voice. Everything still goes through the same reader and planner, so the result is the same toast. It never asks: it adds its best reading. Only when two readings are close does the toast offer the other one as an outline pill. Ignore it and the task stays as added.
