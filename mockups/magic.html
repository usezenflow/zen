Magic, quietly

Zen places your flexible work and leaves it alone. It re-plans only when something happened: the school changed a class, an exam or a due date; you added something or changed a due date; you asked (a rule, "later", a new time); or a block passed undone. Then the change is shown in three sizes: motion when it happens in front of you (rows move one at a time), a trail on each moved row (a vermilion dot and "từ T3 14:00"), and a brief when it happened while you were away. Every sentence on this page is one the app can produce from what it knows.
