Magic, quietly

Zen places your flexible work and leaves it alone. It re-plans only when something happened: the school changed a class, an exam or a due date; you added something or changed a due date; you asked (a rule, "later", a new time); or a block passed undone. Finishing or deleting something frees time; it moves nothing, except that a task still without a time takes it (the toast says “Đọc sách có giờ: T3 16:00”). Then the change is shown in three sizes: motion when it happens in front of you (rows move one at a time), a trail on each moved row (a vermilion dot and "từ T3 14:00"), and a brief when it happened while you were away. Exam and assignment preps (18 onward) are a budget of minutes, not a fixed "2 buổi": a base for the format, multiplied by what Zen knows (credits, a retake, your marks, your pace, what you said last time, a crowded exam week, and what the title and notes say), poured into 60–90 minute sittings named for their step, longer on light days. When the budget changes, the next two days stay as they are and sittings are added or removed further out. Every sentence on this page is one the app can produce from what it knows.
