Magic, quietly

Zen rearranges your week on its own. Nothing here asks you to replan. The diff is shown in three sizes: motion when you open the app (blocks glide from the old time to the new one), a trail on each moved row (a vermilion dot and "from T3 18:00" until you've seen it), and a brief that tells the story when the change was big. Every move has a reason in words, read straight from the model. Long-press puts it back, and that teaches Zen.
